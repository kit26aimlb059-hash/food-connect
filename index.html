Why Food Connect

Make surplus food part of the solution.

Every day, good food is left over while many people face food insecurity. We make it easier for communities to turn that gap into a connection.

  • ✓Purpose: reduce avoidable food waste and make sharing simpler.
  • ✓Vision: a future where every community can access nourishing food.
  • ✓Mission: connect food donors, trusted NGOs and willing volunteers.
Simple by design

Good food moves in three steps.

A clear, community-led flow helps surplus food find the people and organizations that can use it.

1

Donors list surplus food

Restaurants, hotels and event hosts share food details, portions and a safe pickup window.

2

NGOs request a match

Local organizations review available listings and request food that fits their community's needs.

3

Volunteers coordinate

Volunteers can take a pickup task and update its progress through the demonstration workflow.

A shared global goal

SDG 2: Zero Hunger

The United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 2 calls for ending hunger, achieving food security, improving nutrition and promoting sustainable agriculture. Food Connect supports this goal at a local level by helping usable surplus food reach community organizations.
